## Caption (paste verbatim under the post)

```
I shipped Episode 1 of Papers in the Wild this week.

Then I noticed something awkward.

9 hours producing. 0 hours distributing.

This is the part of digital marketing nobody warns builders about. The work is 40% of the job. Distribution is the other 60%.

So this week I am running an experiment on myself:
↳ 15 minutes of outreach before any production work
↳ One direct email to each paper's authors when I cover them
↳ Track distribution metrics, not just production metrics

If you ship creative work and you also skip distribution: tell me your trick. I am taking notes.

The Episode 1 launch post and full writeup are linked in my bio ↓

#digitalmarketing #contentdistribution #buildinpublic #papersinthewild
```
