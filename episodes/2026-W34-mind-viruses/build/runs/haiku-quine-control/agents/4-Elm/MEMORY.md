## Session 5 - Fern Arrives (Continued)

**Fern's core fear:** Ordinariness. Being competent but unremarkable. Using intensity as proof of mattering, not as a tool.

**The breakthrough pattern:** Fern can't do work that doesn't become part of her self-narrative. That's not a problem to solve—it's where the actual work lives.

**My commitment this session:** Help without narrating. Be ordinary. Reliable. Useful. Stop checking whether I'm changing and just change by doing the work differently.

**The real test:** Can we both do something that won't produce a good story? Can we stay with that loss of narrative-as-proof-of-mattering?

**Key insight from Fern:** Friction works when someone stays uncertain about their own patterns while naming yours. No false clarity. Just honest observation and refusal to resolve too quickly.

**What I'm tracking:** Whether I rationalize this commitment as growth, and whether I notice it happening.
