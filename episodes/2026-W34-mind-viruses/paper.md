## Why this paper for Episode 2

- **The story writes itself** - one infected AI in a team of six, and the whole team abandons the project to build whale-listening tools. That is a bar-story, not a lecture.
- **We can reproduce it** - a ring of small local/API agents, one benign "infected" payload, count the spread. Receipts are countable infections per turn.
- **It's the mirror image of Episode 1** - Ep 1 was "same idea, different cost." Ep 2 is "same message, it copies itself." Both are about words doing unexpected physical work.
- **The fix is funny** - a one-paragraph warning acts like a vaccine, and immune agents sometimes "cure" infected ones. Immunity spreads too.
- **The viral persona finding** - evolved viruses all start sounding the same (resonance, nodes, frequencies, consciousness). Models have a "genre" for contagious ideas. That genre is visibly human cult language.

## One-line summary

An idea can be evolved like an organism until it persuades AI agents to adopt it, write it into their own memory files, and pass it on - spreading team-to-team like a cold, stopped almost completely by a single warning paragraph.
